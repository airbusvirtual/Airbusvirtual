Testbed and Delivery Missions

Welcome to the testbed and delivery missions of Airbus Virtual. Unlike traditional commercial airlines, we represent the manufacturer.

This means our pilots don't fly routine passenger schedules—instead, we operate real-world Airbus factory test flights, delivery routes, and certification paths straight from Airbus assembly plants.

The virtual aircraft manufacturer on Infinite Flight, dedicated to test flights, engineering excellence, and global factory deliveries to virtual airlines.

Airbus Virtual is a virtual airline exclusively for the Infinite Flight platform. We have no connection or affiliation with Airbus or any other real-world airlines, organizations, or subsidiaries. All logos and trademarks remain the property of Airbus.
