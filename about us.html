About Us

At Airbus Virtual we live by the motto "We Make It Fly" across the global skies of Infinite Flight. Whether you enjoy short regional hops, medium-haul routes, or ultra-long-haul intercontinental journeys, our operational network spans the entire globe.

Our virtual fleet covers the full spectrum of Airbus aviation available in Infinite Flight, offering incredible variety for every type of pilot.

Airbus Industrie is a multinational aircraft manufacturer, primarily producing commercial and military aircraft. Through the huge variety of Airbus aircraft in Infinite Flight, we are able to offer a vast number of routes, including delivering aircraft to customers, test flights and Airbus World Tours. From A220 to A380, we have the widest range of fleet available for pilots, depending on their rankings in Airbus.

The virtual aircraft manufacturer on Infinite Flight, dedicated to test flights, engineering excellence, and global factory deliveries to virtual airlines.

Airbus Virtual is a non-profit organisation and is not associated, endorsed, or affiliated with Airbus or its subsidiaries.
