Rank Transfer

If you are already flying with another Virtual Airline (for example, as a Captain with 150 hours in ABCVA) and you join our VA, you don't have to start from the bottom as a First Officer. You can directly become a Captain here without re-flying all those hours!
How it works:
1. Open a ticket in our Discord under #rank-transfer.
2. Take a short practical check/test with us.
3. Once you pass, you will directly join at the Captain rank.
Hidden Tip: If you pass your test with exceptional performance and impress our staff, you can skip ahead and directly unlock our top post: Senior Commander (520 hours)!
