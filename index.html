Airbus Virtual
(AIBVA)

Welcome to Airbus Virtual (AIBVA), the community in Infinite Flight dedicated to celebrating the innovation, engineering excellence, and legacy of Airbus. Designed for virtual pilots who value precision, realism, and high-quality flying, our virtual airline brings together aviation lovers from every corner of the world.

Operations:

We have two main forms of operations, which are as below:

Delivery Flights

You can send Airbus aircraft to our customers from several manufacturing locations.

Testbed Flights

You can become the pilots to maneuver the aircraft and make sure they are safe and ready for your customers.

The virtual aircraft manufacturer on Infinite Flight, dedicated to test flights, engineering excellence, and global factory deliveries to virtual airlines.
